The lease at our current building is coming to an end and will not be renewed, so the Dallas Makerspace needs to move. This page will be used to document potential locations for the new space.

## Requirements

- Price range is from \$1000-\$1200 for starters, \$1500 max.
- 0.25\$ per square foot per month, or \$3.00 per square foot per year
- at least 1000 square feet, 5000 square feet would be ideal
- In Dallas County
- Area that's reasonable safe after dark
  - [City of Dallas interactive crime map](http://dallas.dfwmaps.com/crimenew/)
  - [Crime reports by zip](http://dallas.everyblock.com/crime-reports/)
- Near major North-South and East-West highways
- Within walking distance of a DART rail station
- Area where Verizon FIOS or other highspeed internet is available
- Area zoned for use of noisy tools such as welding equipment and saws
- Rooms suitable for meetings, lectures, and classes
- Warehouse-like work area for larger power tools
- Garage doors and area suitable for working on vehicle inside
- Smaller, private meeting rooms
- Restrooms
- Kitchen and social areas
- Heating and cooling
- Sufficient parking
- 220V and/or 480V power outlets for large tools and welders

### Nice To Have

- Darkroom considerations
  - Room with two doors and no windows
  - Room with climate control and ventilated
  - Room with a wet wall, preferably one that backs up to a sink on the other side
