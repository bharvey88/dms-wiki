## CO2 Canister

1.  Spare full (50 lb) canisters are stored in Electrical Room, between a wall and an open door.
2.  Specialty canister dolly is located in Metal Shop. Lift canister with a "bear hug" onto dolly. **Canister must be transported with shroud over valve to prevent deadly accident if canister falls and stem is broken.**
3.  Shut valve closed on empty canister.
4.  Slowly open the outlet valve on the regulator.
5.  Watch the pressure gauges drop to zero.
6.  You will need an adjustable wrench to disconnect & reconnect the regulator.
7.  Shrouds are kept on windowsill behind canister. Use sharpie to mark it "Empty". Place over valve stem on canister before moving from wall cradle.
8.  Put new canister in cradle & rechain. Remove shroud and place on windowsill. Install regulator.
9.  Put old canister on cradle. Chain or rope canister to pipe next to back door. **Canister must be transported with shroud over valve to prevent deadly accident if canister falls and stem is broken.** Return dolly to metal shop.
10. Contact AirGas for pick up and refill. Contact and DMS account number are on the admin wiki.
11. When new canister arrives, mark shroud with letter "F" for "full" and store in Electrical Room between open door and wall.
