## Alyssa Pipe

### Statement of Intent

If elected I intend to upload the bylaws and rules and policies of the Dallas Makerspace. I would encourage members to teach classes on subjects that other members are interested in. I feel that making our space as widely known as possible is integral to our survival and intellectual growth. I would seek to purchase equipment and supplies for the Dallas Makerspace that would serve as many as our members as possible. I will do my best to be as fair and unbiased as possible in all of my decisions.

### Conflict of Interest Statement

I would like to state that although I am a board member of the Dallas Personal Robotics Group that I would not vote on any items as a board member that would be a conflict of interest as per the Dallas Makerspace bylaws.
