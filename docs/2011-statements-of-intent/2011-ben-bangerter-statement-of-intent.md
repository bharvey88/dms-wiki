This letter is to serve as formal notification that I, Ben Bangerter, am officially submitting my name to run for election to the Board of Directors for the Dallas Makerspace for the year 2011. As a life long tinkerer I’m truly exited to be a part of this group. As a board member I would seek policies that are open and democratic. I would also try to foster a culture of openness and opportunity. The collective expertise of our members is a tremendous resource, and I would try to facilitate learning from each other. I promise to serve the interest of the group as a whole and to uphold the spirit and policies of the Dallas Makerspace.
