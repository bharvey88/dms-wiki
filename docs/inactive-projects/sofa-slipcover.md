How do you keep the dogs off the sofa when they have muddy feet?
Trick question! It is both impossible and inevitable! :)
So the next best thing is to protect the sofa with a cover, and to toss it in the washer from time to time, and store it in the linen closet when not required.

Skills involved:
-----------------

- basic sewing machine operation
- heavy duty stitching with heavy weight material (non-clothing)
- scissors (ie: not running with)
