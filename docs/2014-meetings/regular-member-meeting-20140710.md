- [Photography Lab](../photography/photography-lab.md) (Darkroom Committee) - Steve Reeves

- Darkroom - I've begun an informal logistical/feasibility analysis of setting up a darkroom in the new space.

- Is there sufficient demand? Interested parties should email pandabob@gmail.com and let me know they exist.
- Is there any way at all to have a place with water supply AND drainage?
- I currently believe there is a lack of sufficient demand for a dedicated darkroom. However, I do believe there is *some* demand for one from both photographers and screen printers.
- I wonder if it wouldn't be more practical to build a "wet work" room that could double as a darkroom upon occasion. I believe it's noteworthy that we do not have much in the way of a shop sink setup in the workshop area. A place to clean up from projects that's not the kitchen or the bathroom seems desirable.
- Failing demand and/or logistics for a plumbed darkroom, I plan to seek out a place in the building that we can at least make light-tight for limited activity like loading film.
