    Mark, Peter - since you guys are on the lease, I'm hitting you up as
    leaders in DMS.

    I believe we need a DMS appointee to the DPRG board, at least for the
    duration of this startup phase. I envision the role of that person
    being a coordination point for DPRG-related activities such as meeting
    schedules, equipment, the DMS-DPRG relationship, finances, etc. I
    believe in transparency, and I think someone from DMS ought to have
    visibility into the finances that are currently being funnelled
    through the DPRG account, at least until such time as DMS becomes a
    separately acting entity.

    Could you guys please nominate someone who would be willing to join
    the DPRG board as an appointee, until such a role is determined to no
    longer be needed. I understand from Steve you guys have talked about
    this, and I want to see it happen.

    Thanks
