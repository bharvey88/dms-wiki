From: Peter Smith <peters242@gmail.com>
    Subject: [DMS] Now accepting votes for the DPRG-Makerspace Steering Committee
    Date: October 5, 2010 at 2:58:22 PM CDT
    To: dms@dprg.org

    Please vote in the nominees for the 3 new DPRG-Makerspace Steering
    Committee members.  The deadline will be the end of the meeting this
    Thursday (20101007).  Either send your vote to this list or contact a
    member who can give your vote in proxy at the meeting when we hold the
    vote.

    The following Makerspace members are our nominees.

    Eric Chaney
    Mark Havens
    Andrew LeCody
    Steve Rainwater
    Gus Reiter

    To recap, the Steering Committee's purpose is to act as the group that
    can create standing rules (until the by-laws and 501c3 are in place,)
    create sub-committees, coordinate and recommend by-laws, charters,
    etc, and also have the authority to spend money (or delegate out
    moneys) [1]!

    Thanks!
    Peter
