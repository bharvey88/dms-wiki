## Design

The main idea is to have a cooler with a garbage disposal at the bottom to crush the ice and recirculate the liquid and ice.

### Parts

- **NEW** Garbage disposer
- 5 Gallon cooler
- Large bucket
- 3' x 1 1/4" Thin walled PVC pipe
- Ball valve
- 1 1/4" PVC T connector
- 2x 1 1/4" PVC L connector
- 1 1/4" to 3/4" reduction
- 1' 3/4" PVC pipe
- 3/4" PVC L connector

### Tools

- Hack saw
- 3 1/2" Hole saw
- 2" Hole saw
- Pilot bit for hole saws
- Drill
