**Goals of the Brewing Sciences Interest Group:** **\# Provide the equipment and knowledge needed to brew and ferment beers and other beverages**

1.  Teach at least one class per month related to brewing theory or brewing practices
2.  Build a fully functional brewing rig based on the system described at [The Electric Brewery](http://www.theelectricbrewery.com/)
