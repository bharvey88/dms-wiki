## Makerspace video ideas

- Join dallas maker space or I'll kill these humans - a partially completed robot holds dallas makerspace members hostage, threatens to kill them unless more people join and complete the robot.

- Make Dallas Weird - some sort of play on the "Keep Austin Weird" slogan, maybe just a lot of hackerspace members doing their thing, who look up at the camera and say 'make Dallas weird, join Dallas Makerspace'

## DPRG video ideas

- this is your brain on drugs - Show someone with a tiny B9 or R2 toy and say "this is your robot", show a full size B9 or R2 and say "this is your robot when you join the DPRG".
