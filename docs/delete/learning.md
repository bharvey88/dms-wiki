This list is an ever-growing collection of free online learning resources that may be helpful for many people in the hackerspace community.

## Electronics

[All About Circuits](http://www.allaboutcircuits.com/) - Covers DC, AC, semiconductor, and digital electronics

## Digital Signal Processing

[The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to Digital Signal Processing](http://www.dspguide.com/) - Authoritative online book covering DSP in breadth and in depth

## Miscellaneous

[Khan Academy](http://www.khanacademy.org/) - Anything and Everything
[How Stuff Works](http://www.howstuffworks.com/) - Simplistic and terse explanations of everyday items
