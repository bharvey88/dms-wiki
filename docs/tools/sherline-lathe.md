# Sherline lathe

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Sherline lathe](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Sherline_lathe) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

DMS has two similar Sherline microlathes - a model 4000 lathe with 8" bed and a model 4400 with 17" bed and Digital ReadOut (DRO). The model 4000 lathe can be used in either manual or CNC mode, driven by electronically controlled stepper motors. Accessories are interchangeable.

| Hole Through Spindle     | .405″ (10 mm)   | .405″ (10 mm)   |
