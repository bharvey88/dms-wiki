## Minutes

*Minutes are a **mandatory** work product from the meeting and must only include the outcome of actionable agenda items. Adhering to the format below is **strongly** recommended.*

    YYYY-MM-DD [Committee name] MEETING MINUTES

    HH:MM Call to Order

    ----
    ATTENDANCE
    - Attendee 1
    - Attendee 2
    - Attendee 3
    - Attendee 4
    - Attendee 5
    - [list all attendees by full name]

    ----
    Agenda Item 1: (literal text of proposal that emerges from discussion about Agenda Item 1; be concise)
      - For: (count of votes for agenda item 1)
      - Against: (count of votes for agenda item 1)
      - Abstain: (note members abstaining by full name and reason if provided)
    Item disposition (pass/fail)

    ----
    Agenda Item 2: (literal text of proposal that emerges from discussion about Agenda Item 2; be concise)
      - For: (count of votes for agenda item 2)
      - Against: (count of votes for agenda item 2)
      - Abstain: (note members abstaining by full name and reason if provided)
    Item disposition (pass/fail)

    ----
    HH:MM ADJOURNED
