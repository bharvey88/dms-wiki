First Ballot Item \*\*Passes\*\* with \*\*110 votes in favor\*\* and 47 votes against.

- Change Section 4.1.(2) of the bylaws from:

 \* \_Supporting members are not permitted to vote or otherwise influence the decision making process of the corporation.\_

- to

 \* \_Supporting members are not permitted to vote in either the member meeting or in the annual election.\_

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Second Ballet Item \*\*Does Not Pass\*\* with 70 votes in favor and \*\*87 votes against\*\*.

- Change Section 3.1.3(2) of the bylaws from:

 \* \_Any member in good standing shall be qualified to be appointed as an officer of the corporation.\_

- to

 \* \_Any member shall be qualified to be appointed as an officer of the corporation.\_
