## Station Rules

- You must hold a current active amateur radio license to operate the station.
- NO CLUB OR CLUB CALL SIGN EXISTS NOW.
- Please turn all radios and power supplies off when finished operating and disconnect all antenna connection.

### Current Station

## Antennas

We have an MFJ G5RV for the HF radio and a Jpole for the 2M/70CM radio. No antenna for receiver yet.
