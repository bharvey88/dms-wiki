## Events

- As this is the first meeting of the DMS Amateur Radio Committee in a period of time, there are no previous minutes to review or discuss

- Introductions / any projects that people want to share

- Greg - General license - Interest in electronics and SDR
- Pat - Guru and legend - Emergency communications services RACES / Skywarn
- Ben - Recent acquisition of license - Enthusiastic about committee and reviving it

- Wiki Page Update

- Ben would like to remove the constraint that members have to have license

- Part \#97 of rules & regulations states that we have to have a minimum of 4 members with regular meetings and a roster
- We decided to adjust Rule \#1 of Amateur Radio Committee rules to state "includes Amateur Radio licensees"

- This allows additional members who are unlicensed to participate

- Should we transfer club call-sign KD5MS from the Pat Hykkonen to the Amateur Radio Committee head (Benjamin Groves)

- We are able to change our leadership every 6 months, according to DMS bylaws
- A license transfer every 6 months may be cumbersome and annoying
- Trusteeship of the FCC Club License is who FCC comes looking for if there is an issue (the responsible party)

- Pat has experience in handling these issues
- In case of issue on site, Pat is easily accessible

- DECISION : Leave trusteeship of the KD5MS Amateur Radio Club Call-Sign with Pat Hykkonen

- An offer of a repeater parts donation from friends of Steve Adams

- The repeater nice, but seems to be a future project in scope

- Start small

- DECISION : Decision to respectfully hold for now

- Discovery of amateur radio masts in DMS storage

- 6-8 sections originally donated

- We got rid of 4 - (munged)
- We kept 4 that are in decent shape

- They are not heavy duty

- Not a good idea to combine them all into one 40 foot section of tower
- DECISION : Hang onto them till we get to the next Dallas Makerspace location

- See what we can do with them with proper roof access
