# Hackathon Feb 2013

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Hackathon Feb 2013](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Hackathon_Feb_2013) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

**This event has been completed.**
This page will remain here to serve as a record of the event.

Our first official Hackathon for Friday Feb 15th, starting at 7pm.

The goal of this hackathon will be to build a working ping pong ball launcher. If enough people show up, we'll split into teams and see who can build the best launcher.

Teams will be judged on:

- accuracy - Can you hit the broad side of the space?
- reliability/repeat-ability - Is your launcher a one-shot wonder?
- appearance - Gotta look good.
- velocity - If we can get a chronograph in time.

Discussion thread is on the forums: <https://dallasmakerspace.org/forums/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=90>
