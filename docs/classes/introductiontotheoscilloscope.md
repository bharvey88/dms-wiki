## Class Outline

The class will begin with a short lecture/demonstration showing the basic controls of the scope and how they operate. The remainder of the class will consist of the students breaking up into three teams of two people to examine three circuits. You will be given a one page handout that has a brief summary of the location of the various controls and a series of questions concerning each of the sample circuits you are expected to use the oscilloscope to answer.
