Due to the tie for 5th place and lack of a mechanism in the bylaws to resolve ties - and with the consensus of all present - the Secretary opted for a *game of chance* with two die cast for each contestant for the 5th seat. A 12 was cast for Curtis Baker, a 9 was cast for Jim Hartnett, thus Curtis Baker was declared winner.
