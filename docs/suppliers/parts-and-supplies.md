## Electronics

### Catalog Distributors

**CAUTION!** DO NOT buy semiconductors on eBay, Alibaba or UTsource and the like unless you have zero alternative! Counterfeit devices from China are rampant on auction sites and Chinese vendors. If the price seems too good, it probably is. Use an authorized distributor to avoid nasty surprises. [More info on counterfeit devices can be found here.](http://sound.westhost.com/counterfeit.htm)

- [DigiKey Electronics](http://www.digikey.com/)
- [Mouser Electronics](http://www.mouser.com/)
- [Newark / Element 14](http://www.newark.com/)
- [MCM Electronics](http://www.mcmelectronics.com/) - A/V focused but has lots of stuff including hard to find A/V gear parts and assemblies
- [B+D Enterprises](http://www.bdent.com/) - Similar to MCM, has lots of stuff including A/V gear parts.
- [Parts Express](http://www.parts-express.com/) - Almost exclusively A/V, extremely popular with the DIY Audio crowd.
- [Madisound](http://www.madisoundspeakerstore.com/welcome.php) - Nothing but speakers and speaker related things.
- [Sparkfun](https://www.sparkfun.com/) - All kinds of DIY electronic supplies. A great place to start for beginners with lots of Arduinio stuff. Use if the local distributors are out of stock.

### Transformers

- [Toroid Corporation of Maryland](http://www.toroid.com/) - They do quality work. They also sell a semi-custom kit if you want to spec a transformer and wind it yourself to save money.
- [E Craftsmen](http://www.ecraftsmen.com/) - 5 unit minimum on custom transformers and inductors. Looks to be very high quality. Their standard line includes some neat autotransformers for lighting applications.

### Oddball and Hard To Find Items

- [Electronics Plus](http://www.electronicplus.com/) - They are a MG Chemical dealer. MG has long been a reliable supplier when you need FR4 w/ \>1OZ copper. Usually this is PCBs for power supplies or other apps where you use a thicker copper board to keep traces from being too wide for a given current. Also has a variety of fluxes and circuit board repair supplies.
- [All Electronics](http://www.allelectronics.com) A very old and established mail order surplus dealer. Reasonable prices on most things.

- [Electronic Goldmine](http://www.goldmine-elec-products.com/) Another old online surplus shop. \$10 minimum order.

## Robotics

### Actuators

- [Muscle Wires](http://www.musclewires.com/) - Sometimes motors or linear actuators take too much power or won't give you a high enough reliability. Low power high MTBF actuators can be made with shape memory metal when performance is more important than unit cost. This place has muscle wire actuators and an electrically controlled Schrader valve for metering out air from a compressor.
- [Images Scientific Instruments](http://www.imagesco.com/) - They carry a variety of Nitinol actuators.
