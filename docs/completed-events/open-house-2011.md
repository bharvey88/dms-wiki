## Introduction

Dallas Makerspace is holding an open house to introduce the public to our space and the projects we've worked on this year. Projects like high voltage machines, experimental art, 3D printing, lasers, crafts, film and digital photography, and biology. Everyone (that means you!) should come out and meet us, see our space and our tools, and sit in on a few demonstrations. If you've never visited a hackerspace, now's your chance to find out what goes on and get in on the fun. Thanks to some generous donations we also have some contests and prizes lined up. And we're planning a raffle for a year membership to the Dallas Makerspace worth \$600 (1 raffle ticket for \$10, 2 for \$15, 3 for \$20).

- **When:** Saturday, November 5, 2011, from Noon until Midnight
- **Where:** Dallas Makerspace, 2995 Ladybird Ln, Dallas, TX 75220 [Google Maps](http://maps.google.com/maps/place?q=2995%20Ladybird%20Ln,%20Dallas,%20TX%2075220-1415)
- **RSVP:** If you're on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=271254349581324), you can [RSVP and share the event with your friends!](https://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=271254349581324)
