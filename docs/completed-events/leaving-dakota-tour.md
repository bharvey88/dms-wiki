# Leaving Dakota Tour

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Leaving Dakota Tour](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Leaving_Dakota_Tour) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

**This event has been completed.**
This page will remain here to serve as a record of the event.

Dallas Makerspace will be showcasing the work of Philidelphia Photographer, [Kyle Cassidy](http://www.kylecassidy.com/) on Friday, March 11th. The photograph series is 24 images depicting a story titled ['Leaving Dakota'](http://www.kylecassidy.com/projects/dakota/index.html/). He came up with the idea to send the series on tour and called for volunteers around the world to set up a single night gallery showing of the series. He added the twist of asking each volunteer to interpret the story behind the images and create their own to display and send with the series.

Starting in California at the beginning of the year, the tour will make several stops before making it to Dallas for the March showing.
