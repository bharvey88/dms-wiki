# 2011 Open House - Bio Launch

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [2011 Open House - Bio Launch](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/2011_Open_House_-_Bio_Launch) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

**This event has been completed.**
This page will remain here to serve as a record of the event.

The 2011 Open House is done! We had a superb showing, it was a great way to launch the DMS bio program. For a video of the open house, see [this video](http://artandseek.net/2011/11/08/artcon-7-artist-studio-tour-with-haley-moore-video/).

We showed:

- The [Strawberry DNA extraction protocol](../science/strawberry-dna-extraction-protocol.md)
- A [23 and Me](http://www.23andme.com) human DNA test kit, with analysis results and discussion of genomics
- A microscopy demo
- A signup station for the [Flu Near You](https://flunearyou.org/?a=62227164a1ee90) citizen science flu-spotting project
- A [FitBit](http://www.fitbit.com), with a discussion of the [Quantified Self](http://quantifiedself.com/) movement, where people gather data about their metabolism to track their body and investigate their health
