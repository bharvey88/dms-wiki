- A member who has [Heberden's nodes](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heberden%27s_node) was interested in research that condition and the pathways that affect it. We looked for information on any identified contributing genetic markers using [23 and Me](http://www.23andme.com) (none have been identified). We suggested participating in crowd-sourced medical research through [Patients Like Me](http://www.patientslikeme.com), which has a [project on Heberden's nodes](http://www.patientslikeme.com/symptoms/show/12271-heberdens-nodes)
- We need to get a demo together for the [Open House 2011](../completed-events/open-house-2011.md). Activity will begin to focus on this.
- We are putting together a curriculum of lab experiments to get activity started.
- Philip gave a talk on PCR techniques. There is a [virtual lab](http://learn.genetics.utah.edu/content/labs/pcr/) you can do on this.
- Other topics discussed included microfluidics and millimeter scale self-assembly.
- A [TED talk on self-assembly](http://www.ted.com/talks/skylar_tibbits_can_we_make_things_that_make_themselves.html) shows a chain made with self-biased physical pieces which, when you shake it, folds up like a protein. It made is realize we need more macro-scale physical models of molecular machines.
