The Healthometer Body Fat Monitoring Scale is on loan to DMS by Lisa Selk. It was brought in to help members with their weigh-ins as part of the [LifeMakers Weight Loss Challenge 2013 Q1](../completed-projects/lifemakers-weight-loss-challenge-2013-q1.md) and general activities from the proposed [LifeMakers](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/LifeMakers) Commitee.

Currently "user 1" is programmed for Lisa's stats. The other three "users" can be programmed on the fly by anyone who wishes to see what their body fat, etc... is like. Please do not erase Lisa's progress. Below are screen shots of the manual so you can see how it works.
