## **Superconductors**

**YBCO Super conductors:**

We are attempting to make YBCO (yttrium-barium-copper-oxide - YBa2Cu3O7) Superconductors in the [Science Committee](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Science_Committee) at DMS.

**Comprised of the following chemicals in a 1-2-3 ratio:**

- Yttrium Oxide
- Copper (Cupric) Oxide
- Barium Carbonate (TOXIC)

**Equipment:**

- Furnace capable of 1000 Degrees Celsius
- Alumina Crucible
- Oxygen source

**Demonstration:**

- Liquid Nitrogen
- Rare-earth magnets

There are two primary methods for making YBCO: the "Shake and Bake" method and the Pyrolysis method. We are first attempting the Shake and Bake method.
