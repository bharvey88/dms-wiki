    Automated dash cam:  raspberry pi 2, camera, and wifi.  Automatically start recording when car running, automatic shutdown, automatic upload of video when in range of home wifi.  Probably include an arduino to manage power.  System to power up on door open, or ignition on. Recording to stop 10 min past ignition off.  Pi to start offloading video when in range of correct wifi.  After 10 min post ignition off recording and offload complete, pi to halt. Arduino to detect reduction of current and shut itself and pi off hard.  Arduino to run 40 minute fail safe timer and shut down timer even if pi has not halted.
