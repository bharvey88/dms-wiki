This serves as formal notice as required by §4.6.1(5) of the Dallas Makerspace bylaws of my willingness to serve on the Board of Directors of the Dallas Makerspace for the 2019 term.

A vote for me is an explicit authorization - notwithstanding any previous policy or bylaw provision to the contrary - that I am allowed to make my Dallas Makerspace gmail account open to public inspection. I am running on this single issue because I want the mandate for transparancy to be unequivocal.

/s/ Allen Wan, Esq.
