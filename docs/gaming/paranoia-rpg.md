# Paranoia RPG

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Paranoia RPG](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Paranoia_RPG) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

A game of backstabbing, secrecy, and comedy. Paranoia takes place in a dystopian complex run by the Glorious Friend Computer that, while well meaning, is horrendously deranged. Your role as a Troubleshooter is to root out and shoot commie mutant traitors, whilst hiding that YOU are a commie mutant traitor.

If handy bring a d20, some paper, and a pencil can't promise I'll have enough for everyone. Bring a laptop or a device that can run Discord as well. This game has meta-secrecy and as such there are private channels to talk to the GM.

*Expect possible language, explained scenes on how your character was dismembered, and overall chicanery*
