### Project 1

Have to gather parts for the project. Needed: kite, picavet, camera.

Got a kite--going to use a [Gayla delta-wing kite](http://www.amazon.com/Delta-Super-Bat-Kite-Nylon/dp/B002NXS4EQ/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&s=toys-and-games&qid=1273870373&sr=8-1)

Made a picavet--used dowel rods, eyelet-screws, zip-ties, an old CDROM, and hot glue.

Got a camera--[Alan Hatchett](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/User:Patchets) has given us a [Canon Powershot A470](http://www.usa.canon.com/consumer/controller?act=ModelInfoAct&fcategoryid=183&modelid=16338) to run [CHDK](http://chdk.wikia.com/wiki/CHDK) on.
