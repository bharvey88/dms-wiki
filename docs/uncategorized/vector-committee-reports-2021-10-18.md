# VECTOR Committee Reports 2021-10-18

!!! note "Source"

Attendees: Kobin, Raymond, Paul, Stephen

**Agenda:**

7:00 - Meeting begins.

7:05 - Meeting called to order. Only Stephen Wylie wishes to run for chair. Committee votes unanimously to nominate Stephen Wylie as chair.

7:08 - Review of recent events, including the DFW Pinball League IFPA\* Summer Finals (tournament of EMs\*) at DMS on 9/4 (which did bring us at least 1 new member) and Raymond's CRT Monitor Basics & Repair class on 10/6. We raised \$145 from the tournament and made the usual honorarium from the class, which over 30 people attended.

7:15 - Review of future events, including Houston Arcade Expo, TPF, and potentially running more CRT Basics / Bad Caps classes.

7:30 - VECTOR Projects review.

7:38 - Motion to give Jayson Woods 30 days notice before removing his items to Lost & Found, or whatever the proper process is for clearing out personal storage.

7:40 - Adjourn.

**Terms:**

IFPA = International Flipper Pinball Association

EM = Electromechanical pinball machine

TPF = Texas Pinball Festival
