The group is OK with the \$25 monthly allowance for the time being, but wishes to spend the wad on something before it becomes unavailable.

**7:15 PM: Move to allow games to be played by the public in VECTOR.**

CDC guidance indicates transmission risk by touch is minimal. The new committee rule will state that games in VECTOR that are in a playable state are allowed to be played in a socially distant manner (games must be 6 ft apart when in use) and there can only be one player on a game at a time. Players are not allowed to touch the games with anything besides their hands (particularly, this means do not wipe them down before/after use) and players must wash their hands after playing games.

In favor: Unanimous

Opposed: None

*This item will be placed on the Consent Agenda at the next board meeting so that the List of Committee Rules can be updated.*
