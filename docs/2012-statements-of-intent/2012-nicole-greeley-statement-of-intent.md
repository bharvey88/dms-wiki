This letter is to serve as a formal acceptance of nomination for the Dallas Makerspace Board of Directors 2012-2013. I, Nicole Greeley, if elected, will uphold the bylaws put forth and voted upon by members of the DMS. This would include, but is not limited to, upholding the voted upon DMS Standing Rules and policies as well as all preforming the duties of a director as stated in the bylaws. I will, to the best of my ability, keep an open mind and ear to all issues that arise within the organization. I will strive to expand awareness of the orginization and put in time and effort to see that the space is maintained. I am proud to be apart of this organization and hope to continue to help it grow strong.
