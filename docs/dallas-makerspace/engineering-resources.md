Sometimes we mention in the course of conversation "engineering resources", the kind of things professional engineers might use to help them know how to calculate load capacity on a beam, or a circuit, for example. Often we would like to refer back to these resources, especially if they're outside our area of expertise or were mentioned in passing in person. I hope this page can bring some of those together. Please add your own, or those you have heard of.
