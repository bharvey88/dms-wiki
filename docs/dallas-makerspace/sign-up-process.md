# Sign-Up Process

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Sign-Up Process](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Sign-Up_Process) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

1.  They visit the website and sign up. (this is important, this creates their user account)
2.  Have them sign the waiver. (the waivers are in a locker in the entry way, the lower right locker)
3.  Give them an RFID (also in the lower right locker) and activate using this process: <https://dallasmakerspace.org/w/images/0/00/SOP_-_Badge_Registration.pdf>
