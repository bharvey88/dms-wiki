# Warehouse Workbenches

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Warehouse Workbenches](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Warehouse_Workbenches) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

The woodworking side of the Warehouse has 5 workbenches:

- 2 benches in the central area are for general use.
- 2 benches against the east wall are for general tool use and storage.
- 1 bench in the far back corner is for fine woodworking\cabinet making.

## Tool Workbenches

[![Workbench w Slat Rack Shelving](https://dallasmakerspace.org/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Workbench_w_slatracks.jpg/300px-Workbench_w_slatracks.jpg)](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/File:Workbench_w_slatracks.jpg)

The 2 tool workbenches have a [slat rack system for shelving](http://www.woodshopics.com/tool-rack/). There are two methods for attaching objects to the slats:

- Method 1: shelves slip over the slat. 30' of blanks have been cut to customize for tools.
- Method 2: Using a cleat, items lock into the slats.
