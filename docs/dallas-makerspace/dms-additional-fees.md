# DMS Additional Fees

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [DMS Additional Fees](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/DMS_Additional_Fees) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

Non-exhaustive list of classes, consumables, and services with additional fees is provided here.
In the event of a conflict, pricing established by the relevant committee supercedes this document.

## Ancillary Fees & Payments

### Online Payment Links

Online payments (Paypal) may be made Paypal by selecting the appropriate fund [at this link.](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Fund_QR_Codes)

### Classes

Some classes have fees paid at the time of signing up via the Calendar system. Most powered tools have at least an introductory training class required.

### Consumables

Most consumables have fees.
These fees should be paid onsite using the Square kiosks or using the link above.
Consumable prices are dictated by and documented by the relevant committee.

### Tool Usage

Some tools have usage fees.
These fees should be paid onsite using the Square kiosks or using the link above.

## Usage Fees

- Laser Cutter - \$1 per 5 minutes usage fee (rounded up, so 5:01 minutes = 10 minutes of usage).

## Materials Fees

Materials prices are dictated by and documented by the relevant committee.

## Free

- Internet - 500/500 Verizon FiOS
- Computers - Over 8 machines comparable to i7 processors
- Digital Storage - 16TB of total storage available for use by members
- Electrical Power - Virtually unlimited power available
- Air conditioning - 99% of the space is air-conditioned (closets aren't)
- Project storage - Limited timeframe, for large projects
- Personal storage box - Unlimited timeframe, for small projects and personal items and tools
- Classes - many classes are free, especially for members
- Meeting space - free for members (if room is not in use)
- Electronics and robotics tools
- Creative arts tools
- Woodworking tools
- Metalworking tools
- Pottery & ceramics tools (bring your own material)
- Lathe (both metal and wood)
- Blacksmithing (bring your own coal)
- 3D Printing (bring your own filament/resin)
- Vinyl Cutter (bring your own vinyl)
- Unlimited pinball games
