## Elections

This page describes requirements and procedures for Board of Director elections. Committee elections are not included in this information.

### Requirements

The [DMS Bylaws](../dms-official/bylaws.md#section-4-6-1-board-election-meeting) require an election of the Board of Directors at least once per calendar year. Detailed requirements for the Board election are also specified in the [Bylaws](../dms-official/bylaws.md#section-4-6-1-board-election-meeting).

### Procedures

The [current election procedure is described at this link](board-of-directors-annual-election-procedures.md).

### Voting Rights

- The policy regarding member voting rights is presented in the [Rules & Policies](../dms-official/rules-and-policies.md#voting-rights).
- Voting rights must be requested; they are not granted automatically. Clarification is provided in the [New Member 411](new-member-411.md#voting), including how to request voting rights.
