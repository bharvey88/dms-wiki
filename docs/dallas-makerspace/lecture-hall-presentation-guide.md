# Lecture Hall Presentation Guide

## Computer (HDMI)

1.  Turn on the Projector using the Epson remote control
    The projector will "click", then the light will turn on
    The projector screen will drop down after about 15 seconds
2.  Turn on the receiver
3.  Rotate the Input dial on the left to HDMI3 (Computer)
4.  Plug in the white HDMI cable coming from the back of the receiver to your computer
5.  Adjust the volume by turning the right dial on the receiver

When you are done

1.  Disconnect the white HDMI cable from your computer
2.  Turn off the Receiver (upper left button on Receiver)
3.  Turn off the Projector
    Turning off the projector will increase the life of the bulb
    The projector will turn itself off and the screen will automatically retract after 5 minutes
    Wait for it to turn off, or press the power button on the white Epson remote control

## Computer (VGA)

1.  Turn on the Projector using the white Epson remote control
    The projector will "click", then the light will turn on
    The projector screen will drop down after about 15 seconds
2.  Turn on the receiver
3.  Rotate the Input dial on the left to HDMI3 (Computer)
4.  Plug in the white HDMI cable coming from the back of the receiver to the VGA Adaptor
5.  Hook up the VGA Adaptor to your computer
    1.  connect to the VGA port
    2.  connect the USB port for power (may need to use usb extension)
    3.  connect the audio (may need to use audio extension)
6.  Adjust the volume by turning the right dial on the receiver

When you are done

1.  Disconnect the white HDMI cable from the VGA Adaptor
2.  Disconnect the VGA Adaptor and cables from your computer
3.  Put the VGA Adaptor and cables back in the box
4.  Turn off the Receiver (upper left button on Receiver)
5.  Turn off the Projector
    Turning off the projector will increase the life of the bulb
    The projector will turn itself off and the screen will automatically retract after 5 minutes
    Wait for it to turn off, or press the power button on the white Epson remote control
