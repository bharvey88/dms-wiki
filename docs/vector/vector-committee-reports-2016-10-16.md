**Committee Meeting 2016-10-16**

Attendees: Stephen Wylie, Stacy Wylie, Chuck Baber, Patrick Thompson, Shawn Christian, Nicholas Schell, Rodney Black, Raymond Jett, Mary Mathias

Committee chair election held. Chuck Baber unanimously voted committee chairperson.

"VECTOR 2.0" plan discussed to reorganize and refocus the committee. We will implement via a three-step plan...

1\. Re-ignite the social aspect of VECTOR by reorganizing the work area to provide a dedicated open social/interaction space for all DMS members and visitors... while optimizing storage and removing any/all inactive projects and tools.

2\. Actively promote and expand our social media presence. Nicholas Schell is primary contact for this role.

3\. Schedule at least one classe per month (ideally two) across a range of arcade tech topics. Raymond Jett heading this initiative but all will participate.

VECTOR will again be present at the Houston Arcade Expo. Members to attend and bring projects: Nicholas Schell, Stephen Wylie, Stacy Wylie
