**Committee Meeting 2015-7-16**

Attendees - Nicholas Schell, Shawn Christian, Stevo and Stacy, David Smart, Jeremy Ortwine, Jayson Woods, Stephen Brown, Paul Wilson, Doug Emes

Membership is growing. We now have seven active projects on the floor and are at capacity.

Stevo and Stacy are planning a custom pinball platform with servos and new mechs. Will eventually replace Gold Wings.

VECTOR assisted with the setup of the National Videogame Museum in Frisco and should be able to facilitate a pre-opening event. We will notify DMS if/when this occurs and place on the events page.

Paul is now actively working on his Student Price/tiki project... our first re-theme.

Upcoming events which will be attended by VECTOR: Austin retro gaming festival, Pinburgh/ReplayFX, and Houston Arcade Expo.

We are planning to field several machines and finished/functional projects for the DMS open house party on September 12th.

VECTOR is working hard to have a strong showing at Texas Pinball Festival 2016 w/custom booth, finished projects, and displays. Will coordinate additional needs with PR committee.
