Printer Notes

According to <http://makergear.wikidot.com/prusa> setting for retraction: `Plastruder retraction settings: 18mm/m and 1.3mm length` According to <https://reprap.org/wiki/Volumetric_Dimension_settings> setting for extrusion: `MakerGear Mendel Prusa kit: 1258 step/mm experimentally found by moving 100mm and 500mm --Affy, May 15th, 2012`

Progress on Sells Mendel as of 20120302

Paul Brown printed and installed a new [X Carriage](http://www.thingiverse.com/image:100866). It works fabulously well, and also allows the use of the [Budaschnozzle v1.0 hot-end](https://reprap.org/wiki/Budaschnozzle/1.0).

Progress on Sells Mendel as of 20111103

Printed and installed the [Afterthought Foot](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:14112).
