2022 Statement of Intent - Pearce Dunlap

This letter is to serve as formal notification that I, Pearce Dunlap, am officially submitting my name to run for election to the Board of Directors for the Dallas Makerspace for the year 2022.

As part of this Letter of Intent, I also agree to the duties and functions expected of me in this position that include, but are not limited to:

My main goal will be to enable members of the space to pickup and complete projects that will enhance the makerspace for others. Making every effort to ensure that rules are equally and consistently applied throughout the space. If we have a rule that we are not enforcing properly I would seek to change or remove it. Perform the duties and responsibilities of Director as outlined in the Dallas Makerspace Bylaws as well as the Dallas Makerspace Rules and Policies. If elected, I am fully aware and understand the importance of my full participation as a productive and effective leader during my tenure on the Dallas Makerspace Board of Directors, and I agree to the terms and conditions set forth above.

I have a long history at the space and have previously served on the board in 2014, helping to facilitate the move to the current building, served as many chairs, as the logistics officer. and just served as a volunteer for many many hours to keep the space and various tools running.

Endorsements: Andrew LeCody, Adam Oas, Michelle Oas James Henningson, Stephenie Webb, Scott Blevins, Curt Baker
