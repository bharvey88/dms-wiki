# Magic The Gathering

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Magic The Gathering](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Magic_The_Gathering) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

The Boardgame and Magic the gathering group meet every other Saturday evening to play a unique board games and MTG. We love to have both new and experienced players join us whenever they can, so please come by around 8:00pm and find us. Its a bring your own deck but one does not need to bring a board game.

We meet nearly regularly around [release dates](https://magic.wizards.com/en/products/coming-soon) and if you're interested in playing a one on one game then put a message on [Talk](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/c/interest-groups/Tabletop-gaming) so that we can arrange a date and time!
