This page shows you how to get feeds from the DMS Events Calendar to your Gmail calendar or iPhone calendar.

To see the discussion that prompted this wiki page, click this link: [Discussion](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/t/way-to-subscribe-to-dms-events-calendar/13390)

## How to add to my calendar app

1.  Find in your app where to add a vCalendar feed
2.  Use <https://calendar.dallasmakerspace.org/events/feed> as the url
