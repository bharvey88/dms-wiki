The Dallas Makerspace is managed by the Board of Directors, a group of 5 members who are elected once a year. Third Mondays of the month are the preferred date for monthly board meetings. Check the [Dallas Makerspace Events Calendar](http://dallasmakerspace.org/blog/calendar/) for dates and times. Meeting agendas and minutes can be found in the [Meetings Category](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Category:Meetings).

The Board of Directors is responsible for electing [Officers](../officers/officers.md) of the Dallas Makerspace.

For more information on how the Dallas Makerspace is organized, please read the [Bylaws](bylaws.md) and [Rules and Policies](rules-and-policies.md).
