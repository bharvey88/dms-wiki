## Do's and Don'ts

1.  Glass Tools and some supplies are available in the wooden cabinet drawers, below all the glass.
2.  Please clean up after yourself when you are finished.
3.  Grinders must be cleaned and rinsed in the janitorial sink with the clay trap.
4.  Use caution when putting away a hot soldering iron.
5.  The sheet glass and large scraps in the big wooden cabinet are for class use only
6.  Small scrap is for sale - \$1 for 10 sqin. See the sign by the bin
