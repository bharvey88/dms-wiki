!!! note "Source"

## Background Information

We received this robot as a donation and intend to have fun with it!! We found out the \[[london hackerspace](https://wiki.london.hackspace.org.uk/view/Equipment/Staubli)\] has one of these and thanks to them have used their manuals to help start it up.

## Recent Updates

We keep a log on our Talk discussion page [Arm Donation](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Help:Contents%7CRobotic)
