## Monthly Allowances

Monthly Allowances are basically monthly deposits into the Committee from the General Fund. The purpose of allowances is to provide a livable wage to the Committee. Additional funds can be acquired through [honorarium](../dms-official/rules-and-policies.md#honorarium-rules) and donations.
