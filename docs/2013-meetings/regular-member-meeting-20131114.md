**DMS Spending Guidelines** (Ken Purcell)

DMS should craft and adopt a spending policy to support current and future needs. Aggressive spending could force future cutbacks at critical times while under spending deprives the membership of useful tools and services that could be put to productive use. A clearly defined spending policy helps to resolve these competing goals. Continued fast membership growth cannot serve as means to ignore future financial needs.

**DMS Spending Transparency** (Ken Purcell)

Financial transactions must be transparent to the membership. DMS should form a financial reconciliation committee to do a yearly audit of the closed fiscal year. The committee should be made up of regular members who do not have spending authority. The committee would prepare an independent report to present to the Board of Directors and the membership.
