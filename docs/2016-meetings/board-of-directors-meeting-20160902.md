## Time, location

19:02, Google Hangouts

This was an emergency meeting called by Robert Davdison.

## Agenda

### Managed Print Services

**Problem**: There exists an opportunity to save money by purchasing the multi-function printer approved at the last board meeting vs leasing

**Solution**: Pass a resolution of the Board of Directors to modify resolution [(5.8) Managed Print Services](board-of-directors-meeting-20160828.md#managed-print-services-28brooks-scharff-29) to purchase the multi-function printer as opposed to lease.

### Remove Allen Wan as a Procurement Officer

**Problem**: Allen Wan has requested to be removed from the list of Procurement Officers due to his involvement with Finance Committee.

**Solution**: Remove Allen Wan as a Procurement Officer.

### Coffee machine

**Problem**: The member-operated coffee machine is being removed.

**Solution**: Allocate an allotment to rent a K-Cup machine.
