## Minutes

    Meeting called to order ~20:00

    AGENDA ITEM: Woodshop Suspensions and Bans
    -----------------------------------------------------
    •  Andrew Z. handed out copies of DMS Wood Shop Rules to be promulgated and adopted.  They are:
       1.  Completion of Wood Shop Basics is required before using any tool
       2.  Protective eye wear must be worn at all times
       3.  All operators must wear closed toe shoes
       4.  Inspect all tools before use and report any machine malfunctions
       5.  Contact Wood Shop committee member or users manual for tool assistance
       6.  Secure loose clothing and long hair before operating machinery
       7.  Be sure to turn on dust collection and ventilation before powering on machines
       8.  Ear protection should be worn when working around power tools
       9.  Clean all tools and work areas immediately after use
       10. Use your brain and be considerate to all members and tools

    •  While any Wood Shop committee member is always welcome and expected to help others and point out Wood Shop rules and best practices, once these official rules are posted/promulgated,
    certain senior Wood Shop members (list TDB) will be authorized to enforce them in an excellent way
    •  Wood  Shop email address should be added to any signage or official Wood Shop postings or communications
