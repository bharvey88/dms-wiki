Thanks to dataforge to [rebooting their UUCP tier 1 nodes](https://boingboing.net/2019/03/25/tilde-servers-r-us.html). VCC is getting provisioned access to UUCPNET. We'll use this for the pdp-11(sys-v) instances on the communitygrid for mail, news, listserv, fidonet, and basic/pascal/fortran programs. This will also be used to interact with the computer museum in Seattle (sdf!) and as part of a whitepaper the committee will be publishing in 2600 on darknets.
