As the "catch-all" committee, Operations has slowly been accumulating roles. Below is a list of the roles Operations currently fills:

- Storage
- Supplies (paper towels, TP, soap, cleaning supplies, etc)
- General tools
- Snacks (food and drinks)
- General safety - First Aid, Fire extinguishers, cleared walkways
- Trash/recycling
- Network
- Computers
- Servers
- Security cameras
- Website (main, forums, wiki, etc)
- Billing (WHMCS)
- Access control (RFID, makermanager)
- Electrical
- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Compressed air

In order to better handle management of these items, we need to either spin off new committees or place individuals in charge of specific roles (similar to a "sub-committee"). In general, decentralization/delegation is preferable.

## Ideas/Suggestions

### Split into Logistics and Infrastructure

#### Logistics

- Storage
- Supplies (paper towels, TP, soap, cleaning supplies, etc)
- General tools
- General workareas
- Snacks (food and drinks)
- General safety - First Aid, Fire extinguishers, cleared walkways
- Trash/recycling

#### Infrastructure

- Network

- Servers
- Security cameras
