## Committees and Special Interest Groups (SIGs)

This is where stuff happens. As a non-profit, DMS organizes ourselves organically. We're sort of a collection of smaller groups each centered around certain types of fabrication. To get the most use, the key is to figure out which committees and special interest groups are relevant and get involved with these subgroups.
