The logical way to select a space is draft requirements then search to find spaces that match those requirements. The following is a draft of the items to consider in selecting a space. Ideally these elements will be noted as 'Absolutely necessary, make or break' or 'Nice to have, considered as part of a weighted average of desirability.' Some criteria will have elements of each with a certain level of characteristic being absolutely necessary and levels above that being nice to have.

- Amount of space - Square footage to be determined by analysis of the mission/vision for the space developed by the board and consultation with the individual room committees.
- Cost of space - Minimum target with a maximum level
- Suitability of space to mission
  - Location related
    - Impact on membership recruiting - The status or neighborhood of some spaces will improve the ability to attract and retain members and will shape the characteristics of the membership
    - Location
      - Neighborhood - A safe neighborhood is desired given access is 24x7
      - Neighbors
      - Support functions
        - Food
        - Stores (craft electronics, hardware, technology)
        - Other
      - HAM radio concerns
    - Parking
    - Public Transit
  - Internal structure
    - Rooms
    - External space (BBQ/event)
    - Bathrooms
    - Number of floors
    - Hall size
    - Plumbing/Floor drains
      - Large Deep Sinks (Restaurant wash sinks)
      - Darkroom (with plumbing)
      - Eye wash station
    - Aesthetics
    - HVAC
    - Windows and sun exposure
    - Signage
    - Amount of modifications required
  - Access doors
    - Number
    - Size
    - Type
    - Dock
  - Utilities
    - Internet access and speed (preferably at ***least*** 30mbps download with 5mbps upload) OR ability to have line-of-sight to [THIS TOWER](https://maps.google.com/maps/ms?msid=207575452460522027662.0004bd6c058b580e5de99&msa=0&ie=UTF8&ll=32.84015,-96.852894&spn=0.066417,0.132093&t=m&z=14&vpsrc=6&iwloc=0004bd6c4b3c47899fc98)
    - Power (220, 110, 220 three-phase)
    - Gas
    - Water (inside/outside)
    - Waste
    - Recycling
  - Makerspace specific functionality
    - Hours of access
    - Roof access
    - Ceiling access (dust control, air hoses, wiring)
    - Permitting
      - Noise
      - Smells
      - Emissions
    - External & landscaping
- Financial and ownership issues
  - Lease versus buy versus build
  - Lease duration and extension options
  - Solvency of landlord
  - Flexibility of landlord
