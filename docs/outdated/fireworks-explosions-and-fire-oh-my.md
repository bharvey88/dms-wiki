From the 2011/02/10 General DMS meeting, some interest was shown in making fireworks. Here's some information on how we can be legal and safe while delving into some dangerous endeavors.
First, and foremost, we need to abide by the rules and regulations set forth by the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF). You can download this document at [\[1\]](http://www.atf.gov/publications/download/p/atf-p-5400-7.pdf)
