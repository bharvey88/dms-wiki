## Cost Of Recycling Service

### Bluebonnet

- The smallest recycle dumpster is a 2 cubic yard container and we dump it 1xwk.
- Base price \$35.00 per month plus a 6% fuel surcharge, a 5% city fee and tax at 8.25% for a total of \$42.17 per month.
- There is a one-time charge of \$25.00 plus tax if you need casters.
