Dimensions:

    6 ft wide
    30" deep
    10 ft tall
    5 levels

1st quote from secdfw.com was for \$190 (with particle board) and \$140 (without particle board).

## Pallet Rack

The best deal I've found on craigslist so far is \$39 per upright (the things on the left side of the picture) and \$19 per beam. That's \$190 (beams) + \$78 (uprights) + \$ for particle board = ~\$300 per shelf.
