The basic idea is to use a USB webcam affixed to a gooseneck to display documents, hands free closeup work, etc.

The Microsoft LifeCam series does an excellent job and comes in two flavors:

- 720p LifeCam Cinema \$45 <http://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B002MCZJ78>
- 1080p LifeCam Studio \$50 <http://www.amazon.com/Microsoft-LifeCam-Studio-1080p-Webcam/dp/B0042X8NT6>

Will attach it to a pistol-grip swivel head:

- \$28 <http://www.amazon.com/Sunpak-620-CPG-Pistol-Grip-Tripod/dp/B004U5U1BO>
- Other Sunpak models with similar name, but doesn't have 3 levels.

There are basically two choices for the neck, to attach the pistol grip to:

- 11" magic arm \$24 <http://www.amazon.com/FMA-1-Variable-Friction-Adjustable-Kamerar/dp/B005HQ5O8K>
- 2' flexi-arm \$25 <http://www.amazon.com/Manfrotto-237HD-Heavy-Duty-Super-Clamp/dp/B00009R6AY>

The Desk clamp part...

- The clamps! \$23 <http://www.amazon.com/PEDCO-P-UCA-Pedco-UltraClamp-Assembly/dp/B000ABB4HC>

(Nevermind) Mount to a (modified, hehe) gooseneck lamp:

- 4' floor standing \$50 <http://www.amazon.com/WP48-BLK-Goos-FLR-Lamp/dp/B00102DU2M>
- 17" desk \$40 <http://www.amazon.com/Normande-Lighting-GS5-831-Brushed-Steel-2-Inch/dp/tech-data/B0029ZBL02>
