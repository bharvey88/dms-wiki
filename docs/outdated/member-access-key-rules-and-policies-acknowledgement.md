This page is dedicated to the development of a Member Agreement which serves as an acknowledgement and education of the rules for new members.

## Old

1.  I agree to follow all standing rules found at <http://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Rules_and_Policies> and the bylaws at <http://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Bylaws>
2.  I agree to maintain to the best of my ability a safe, secure, and clean environment for myself, other members, personnel, staff, and visitors.
3.  I agree to take all necessary safety precautions when using tools, equipment, materials, and facilities.
4.  I agree not to deface or mistreat Dallas Makerspace facilities or property; and to hold the property of other members to the same standards.
5.  I understand that some equipment is restricted to certified members and agree not to use such equipment until certified.
6.  I understand that the Access Key is for my exclusive use and will not be loaned or transferred.
7.  I will not alter, deface, or duplicate the Access Key in any manner.
8.  I will immediately report the loss of my Access Key.
9.  I understand that the Access Key is Dallas Makerspace property and will return it when my membership is terminated.
10. I understand that failure to comply with the above requirements or standing rules may result in forfeiture of membership.
11. I understand that by accepting the Access Key and signing below I am entering a contract with Dallas Makerspace as stated above.

## New

1.  I agree to follow all standing rules found at <http://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Rules_and_Policies> and the bylaws at <http://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Bylaws>
2.  I agree to maintain to the best of my ability a safe, secure, and clean environment for myself, other members, personnel, staff, and visitors.
3.  I understand that some equipment is restricted to certified members and agree not to use such equipment until certified.
4.  I understand that all members are required to "Be excellent to each other".
5.  I understand that I must directly supervise all my guests at all times that the guests are in the space.
6.  I understand that minors 15 and under must be accompanied by Parent or legal guardian at all times.
7.  I understand that the Access Key is for my exclusive use and will not be loaned or transferred.
8.  I understand that failure to comply with the above requirements or standing rules may result in forfeiture of membership.

#### Comments

- The document needs to inform the reader of the code of conduct and our most important rules.
- "may result in forfeiture" seems too soft
- Number 2 and 3 in "Old" can be combined.
- We don't really care if access cards are defaced. They're worth less than \$1 and it can confuse things when multiple people get the same badge.
- "Old" has no mention of guests
