## Current Plan

The [Board of Directors](../dms-official/board-of-directors.md) have authorized up to \$1,000 for the electrical build out. Our current plan includes adding 3x 20A 110V, 3x 30A 220V and 1x 50A 220V circuits to the warehouse.

### Phase 1: Panel Upgrade

Before any other work can be performed, the main electrical panel needs to be replaced. We have contracted with a master electrician who will be replacing our current panel with a new single phase 200A Square D (QO style) panel. This work will require power to be turned off for 2 to 4 hours, likely during the day.

The panel has already been purchased and is awaiting installation, which is planned for Thursday, May 10th.

### Phase 2: Additional Warehouse Circuits

In no particular order, the following circuits will be installed:

- 2x 20A 110V circuits along the west wall, similar to the setup on the east wall
- 1x 20A 110V circuit overhead, running east to west, roughly in line with the bay door (for retractable extension cords)
- 1x 30A 220V circuit for the cabinet saw, along the north wall on the woodworking side
- 1x 30A 220V circuit for future use, also along the north wall on the woodworking side
- 1x 30A 220V circuit for the kiln, along the north wall on the metalworking side
- 1x 50A 220V circuit for the welder and electric cars, along the west wall

### Additional Phases

Additional steps to take have not yet been determined.

## Ideal Electrical Layout

### Warehouse/Workshop

The warehouse is used for much more than just storage, we have tools for woodworking, metalworking, welding, vehicle maintenance/mad-sciencery, and a kiln.

#### East Wall

Minimum requirements:

- 2x 20amp 110v circuits
- 2-outlet or 4-outlet boxes every 5 feet, on alternating circuits
- 1x 220v circuit, with outlet location to be decided

Ideal:

- 2x 20amp 110v circuits
- 4-outlet boxes every 5 feet, on alternating circuits
- 2x 220v circuits, with outlet locations to be decided

#### North Wall

Minimum requirements:

- 1x 20amp 110v circuit
- 1x 4-outlet box on each side of the storage area

Ideal:

- 1x 20amp 110v circuit
- 1x 4-outlet box on each side of the storage area
- 1x 220v circuit, with an outlet on each side of the storage area

#### West Wall

Minimum requirements:

- 1x 20amp 110v circuit
- 2x 4-outlet boxes
- 1x 220v circuit, with outlet location to be decided

Ideal:

- 2x 20amp 110v circuits
- 2-outlet or 4-outlet boxes every 5 feet, on alternating circuits
- 2x 220v circuits, with outlet locations to be decided

#### South Wall

No minimum requirements at this time.

#### Ceiling

Minimum requirements:

- 2x retractable overhead extension cords, location to be decided

Ideal:

- 2x 20amp 110v circuits
- 4x retractable overhead extension cords
