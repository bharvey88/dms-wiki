## Options

"Shopping for electric service for your business is different from shopping for your residential service. For many businesses, shopping for a Retail Electric Provider is a negotiated purchase process, similar to the purchase of a large quantity of computers or wholesale materials for manufacturing or business operations." - [Power To Choose](http://www.powertochoose.org/_content/_resources/business_info.asp)

[List of Non-residential Providers](http://www.powertochoose.org/_content/_compare/companylist.aspx?view=nonres)

### New Info (May 2014)

- Think Energy - 5.5 cents per KWH for a 24 month term
  - Not rated
- Tara Energy - 5.49 cents per KWH for a 24 month term
  - 4 stars (also has "no customer/base/meter charge" and "100% swing tolerance")
- Summer Energy - 6.06 cents per kwh for a 24 month term
  - 3 stars
- Source Power and Gas - 7.13 cents per KWH for a 12 month term
  - 5 stars
- Cirro - 5.59 cents per KWH for 12 and 24 months
  - 5 star reviews (they also say "no customer/base/meter charge" and "100% swing tolerance")
- Stream Energy - says they cannot beat 5.5 cents

Note: Stars = industry scorecard reviews: <http://www.powertochoose.org/scorecard/Scorecard.pdf>
