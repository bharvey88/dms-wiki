The cooperative project is designed to be the implementation of a single idea or set of ideas by a large portion of the Entrepreneurship group with the interest of creating a reputation building success with financial returns. As yet the legal entity and shares structures have not been selected.

## Participants

The participants listed here are interested in moving forward a group project and feel that a single large project rather than numerous individually owned projects is the best way to use the creativity of the Makerspace members in the commercial realm. Note: People listed here have not committed to any specific task or set of tasks.
