# Textile Hacking Group

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Textile Hacking Group](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Textile_Hacking_Group) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

The Textile Hacking Group is a new group at the makerspace. We are interested in printable fabrics, soft circuitry, clothing hacks, lasered fabric, and the craft of working with textiles and yarn.
