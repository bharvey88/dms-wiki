This page contains a more readable version of the threading, feed, and worm gear cutting charts that are in the manual on page 13.

## Basic change gear information

The Colchester's change gears are 16 diametral pitch, with a 14 1/2 degree pressure angle. The stock gears are 0.5" thick and have a octal keyed bore with a primary diameter of 0.8125". The eight keyways are 0.175" wide and 0.03125" deep.
