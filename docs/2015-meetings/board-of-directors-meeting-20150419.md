## Consent Agenda

Newly proposed agenda items appear in the consent agenda. Any member present at the meeting may pull an item out of the consent agenda for discussion, at which point it goes under "New Business". If nobody objects to the solutions presented by the consent agenda, everything in the consent agenda passes by consensus. Items that have more than one proposed solution should be moved to the "New Business" section.

Any member may edit this wiki page and place an item on the agenda. However, discussion of agenda items should be done via the forums. The cut-off time for addition to or revision of the consent agenda's items is 48 hours prior to the meeting. Please note, agenda items (Consent, New, or Old) that request spending \$250 or more must include a section on "Relevance to our tax exempt purpose".

### Acquire Y-Axis CNC Lathe (Bryan Gangwere)

**Problem:** While the HAAS VF-2 VMC is a valuable tool and serves to drive membership growth, it is limited in the types of parts that it can make. Many parts require a lathe, and while we have a manual lathe, a [Y-axis CNC lathe](http://www.mmsonline.com/articles/why-y-axis-for-turnmill-machines2) allows vastly more complex parts to be made [quickly](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wH-R0ud2Tew) and [accurately](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dwg4pESzBdw). In addition, having a Y-axis lathe would serve to further drive membership growth.

**Solution:** Get financing for or start setting aside funds to purchase a [HAAS ST-10Y](http://www.haascnc.com/mt_spec1.asp?id=ST-10Y&webID=YAXIS_LATHE#explore&gsc.tab=0) CNC lathe ([\$120,000](https://dallasmakerspace.org/w/images/2/21/HAAS_ST-10Y_Quote.pdf) new, including misc costs such as rigging, electrical, tooling, etc). Having funds available should a used lathe come up for sale would be ideal: ([This lathe](http://www.ebay.com/itm/Haas-ST-10Y-CNC-Turning-Center-with-Live-Tooling-and-Rhinobar-Feed-/261808703681?pt=LH_DefaultDomain_0&hash=item3cf503c8c1) would be over \$106,000 purchased new, not including the bar feeder.)

**Relevance to our tax exempt purpose:** Allows us to train members in new skills.

**Solution:** Allocate \$500 to auto committee for purchase of a new rolling step ladder with weight actuated castors that will replace the existing step ladder.

**Details:** Looking at getting a [4 step ladder such as this one](http://www.amazon.com/Tri-Arc-KDSR104166-Industrial-Warehouse-Perforated/dp/B004SIIAPW), the current blue one is 5 steps.
