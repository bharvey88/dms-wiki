- [Financial Committee](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Financial_Committee) - Benjamin Groves
- Busy month in accounting:
  - Immediately after processing June books in July, we transitioned from Quickbooks to Quickbooks Online
    - This will provide us a huge benefit to accessibility and timely reports
    - A major hurdle behind our old method (processing an entire month at a time) can be done in a more live fashion
    - This has incurred a large up front effort, specifically the manual handling 2.5 months worth of duplicate entries
      - Committees did not receive transaction statements for June - Please see Benjamin Groves if you wish for a copy to be made for your group
    - Quickbooks online has different rules for account structure and there are open questions on how we migrated (Credit Cards and PayPal)
    - July books should be processed as usual for the August Board of Directors Meeting, and we should be looking forward to the return of less overhead from there
  - We are working with an external accounting firm for handling of our 2014 taxes and an annual audit of our books
    - This is fortuitous as the external is reviewing our Quickbooks Online work, which will help resolve the above open questions
    - We filed for an additional tax extension, which gives us until September - Not a large concern as our tax effort is afoot
- The verdict on the health of our General Fund is still open
  - Prepare for continued tight budget and limited spending
