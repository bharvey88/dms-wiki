#### Interactive Computer Museum report

The committee has been donated a Payphone for use as a project. The computer committee has agreed to use it for building a Raspberry PI Cluster inside it and having it work with the VOIP phones around the space.

A future event will be scheduled to map out working payphones around the Dallas area and collect numbers to arrange a dial in conference which will be published in [2600 Hackers Digest](http://2600.com).

#### Consent Agenda

Newly proposed agenda items appear in the consent agenda. Any member present at the meeting may pull an item out of the consent agenda for discussion, at which point it goes under "New Business". If nobody objects to the solutions presented by the consent agenda, everything in the consent agenda passes by consensus. Items that have more than one proposed solution should be moved to the "New Business" section.

Any member may edit this wiki page and place an item on the agenda. However, discussion of agenda items should be done via the forums. The cut-off time for addition to or revision of the consent agenda items is 48 hours prior to the meeting. Please note, agenda items (Consent, New, or Old) that request spending \$200 or more must include a section on "Relevance to our tax exempt purpose".

##### Discussion

- [User:Narkane](https://dallasmakerspace.org/w/index.php?title=User:Narkane&action=edit&redlink=1) has a goal to raise \$2000 towards the committee before elections. While [User:Denzuko](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/User:Denzuko) has set a milestone of \$300,000.00 from grants, live stream pledges, patron subscribers, services, and fund matching donations.

- [User:Denzuko](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/User:Denzuko) has also set a challenge to the committee to strengthen recruitment efforts with a contribution matching donation towards bug bounty funds if the committee can regularly release code for DMS and committee projects each quarter. Further more, publishing opportunities have been presented to the committee if these goals are met before the years out.

- Committee agreed that efforts should be placed on focusing towards having content for patreon, blog, and social media. While more outward efforts towards meetups and hack-a-thon events such as coding dogo, ctf, and hardware hacking should be the committee focus on top of the STEM Program and programming services to Dallas Makerspace and members. To achieve this the committee will potion the board for a regular operations budget and setup the Patreon Account, Paypal Account, Linkedin, Volunteer Match, and Coinbase accounts in a way that is supported by [Category:Finance Committee](https://dallasmakerspace.org/w/index.php?title=Category:Finance_Committee&action=edit&redlink=1). The committee will hire a regular article writer to produce blog articles on Dallas Makerspace blog and hackaday which are educational and correlate with the committee mission.
