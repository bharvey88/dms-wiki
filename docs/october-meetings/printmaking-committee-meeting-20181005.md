### Discussion

Discussion was held to help decide on option for screen press purchase. Options included:

1.  Purchase of 6x2 Riley Hopkins Jr. Press
2.  Purchase of 6x4 GSG repossessed (used) press
3.  Solicit donation through Ryonet Cares program - Opens Januaray

Purchase of 6x2 Riley Hopkins Jr. Press - Failed

Solicit donation through Ryonet Cares program when open - Unanimous

Purchase of Flash dryer after expansion, provisional \$600 budget before shipping - Unanimous
