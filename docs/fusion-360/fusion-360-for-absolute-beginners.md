If you are new to Autodesk Fusion 360 and would like to learn it on your own, you are on the right page.

## Where to Get Fusion

This address may change, and it may be best to do a Google search. But for now, go to the [Fusion 360 Site](https://www.autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/overview) and [download a free Trial](https://www.autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/free-trial). Once you have your free trial, in a few days, you can convert it for use for more than 30 days by following these steps:

1.  Open Fusion 360.
2.  Click on the countdown timer in the upper right.
3.  Convert from a trial to a enthusiast user. This allows you to use Fusion 360 indefinitely as long as you follow the restrictions in the description.
