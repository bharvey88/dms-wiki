# Brother KH-930e

!!! note "Source"
    Mirrored from [Brother KH-930e](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Brother_KH-930e) on the Dallas Makerspace wiki (CC BY-SA 3.0).

The Brother KH-930e is a computer-controlled electronic knitting machine. Up to 200 stitch; repeat patterning with single motif. 550 built in patterns. You can also design your own patterns via a black and white button system.
