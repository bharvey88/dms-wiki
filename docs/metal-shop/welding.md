### Consumables

#### Inert Welding Gasses

Welding gas is stored outside the building in a cage on the east side. Information for using the gas is [here](https://talk.dallasmakerspace.org/t/metal-shop-welding-gas-moving-outside/64168/15) in hon1nbo's post on Talk.

Bottles are currently available for remote welding.

#### TIG Welding

The Dynasty and Synchrowave use a 'type 9 / 20' torch head, so you need consumables that fit that head type. In general the torches are equiped with a gas lens setup and \#7 cup, but it is recommend you provide your own consumables and tungsten.

Local welding shops can provide them if you tell them what type of torch head we use.

In addition, there are multiple online sources:

- [Amazon - Gas Lens and Accessory Kit](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07PVYR8ZW)
- [US Weld - Standard Alumina Nozzels](https://www.usaweld.com/TIG-Torch-Replacement-Parts-9-17-18-20-26-Series-s/85.htm)
- [US Weld - Clear Pyrex kits](https://www.usaweld.com/Pyrex-Cup-Parts-for-9-20-Series-TIG-Torches-s/68.htm)

#### MIG Welding

MIG Wire is provided, charged at a rate of \$1 per 3 minutes of welding.
