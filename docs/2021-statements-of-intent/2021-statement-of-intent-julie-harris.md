My name is Julie Harris, Julie-Harris on Talk, and this is formal notice of my intent to run for reelection to the board of directors for Dallas Makerspace. I have been a member of Dallas Makerspace since 2016 and I served on the board for the past two terms.

Currently, I am self-employed, working as a fractional controller and consultant for several local clients. I have more than fifteen years of experience in accounting that includes a mix of public and private accounting. I have extensive experience cleaning up financials, increasing department efficiency, developing policies and procedures, managing expense reports and credit cards.

**Accomplishments over the past two terms include:**

- Recovery from negative cash flow and improved cash management. As a result, Dallas Makerspace is in a strong position to not only survive the pandemic but to also complete the expansion despite a significant decrease in revenue due to COVID-19.
- Effectively responding to challenges presented by COVID-19 over the past year resulting in a successful reopening
- Increased professionalism and efficiency of board meetings
- Establishment of a management structure to separate the board from day to day operations and increase accountability

**Goals for the next term:**

- Recruit new members
- Continue to support expansion
- Improved communication of volunteer tasks and recognition of volunteers
- Improved onboarding for new members
- Support PR and fundraising efforts

Endorsements: Curt Baker, Justin Walker, James Henningson
