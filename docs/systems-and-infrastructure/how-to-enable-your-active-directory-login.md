## Several Dallas Makerspace resources are accessed through your Active Directory (AD) login account.

### Active Directory Is Used To Access These Resources

- **JUMP server** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/JumpServerFAQ>)
- **General use laptops** (located in the Multipurpose Room/Room 1/"The Common Room")
- **Wiki** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Main_Page>)
- **Maker Manager** (<https://accounts.dallasmakerspace.org/makermanager>)
- **Calendar** (<https://calendar.dallasmakerspace.org>)
- **Blog** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/wp-admin>)

I don't think these are available any more:

- **Voting** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/voting>)
- **Dallas MakerSpace IM** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/IM_Server>)
- **Dallas MakerSpace Group Chat/Conference** (<https://dallasmakerspace.org/conf>)

### Create/enable your Active Directory (AD) login account through WHMCS (aka "the billing system")

NOTE: *Only members with an account in WHMCS will be able to do this, at this time.* NOTE: *Add-on (AKA "Family Members") accounts are managed through Maker Manager*

- Log in at: <https://accounts.dallasmakerspace.org/accounts/clientarea.php>
- In the top-right corner, click the drop down menu that says "Hello, YOURFIRSTNAME!"
- Click "Edit Account Details"
- Take note of your username near the bottom. That is the username of your LDAP/AD account (once created). DO NOT ATTEMPT TO CHANGE YOUR USERNAME!
- Correct any personal information if necessary.
- Click "Save Changes" at the bottom, even if you made no changes.
- Go back to the top-right corner ("Hello, YOURFIRSTNAME!") and click your name on the dropdown menu again
- Click "Change Password" and enter in your current password in all three fields. At this time, special characters are not supported.
- Click the blue "Save Changes" button.
